CAS 1 – Participant soumet un score

score1

score2

winnerRegistrationId

submittedByUserId

CAS 2 – Les deux participants confirment

Si :

confirmedByParticipant1 = true

confirmedByParticipant2 = true

CAS 3 – Admin confirme

status = COMPLETED

recalculateGroupStandings(groupId)
